# AQF-T C-016: Knowledge Lifecycle Boundary

Version: V1.0.0 | Status: ✅ FROZEN — Constitution-Level
Date: 2026-07-28 | Source: V3.1-006 Knowledge Lifecycle V1.0

---

## Core Definition

**Knowledge is a validated hypothesis, not permanent truth. Knowledge influences decisions. It never overrides core rules.**

---

## C-016-1: Knowledge ≠ Rule

Knowledge MAY influence Decision confidence and weights. Knowledge SHALL NOT override Core Decision Rules, Risk Rules, or Immutable Core.

---

## C-016-2: Knowledge Provenance Mandatory

Every Knowledge entity MUST carry: Source, Evidence Level, Validation Count, Applicable Regime, Confidence. Per MC-001.

---

## C-016-3: Retired Knowledge Isolation

Retired Knowledge MAY be preserved for research. SHALL NOT influence real-time trading decisions.

---

## Lifecycle States

DISCOVERED → VALIDATING → CONFIRMED → ACTIVE → DECAYING → RETIRED → ARCHIVED

Active → Decision influence allowed. Decaying → Reduced weight. Retired → No influence.
