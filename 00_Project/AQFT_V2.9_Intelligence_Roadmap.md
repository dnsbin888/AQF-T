## Design Principles (V2.9+)

All V2.9 modules must pass six review gates:

1. **Architecture Review** — Consistent with system architecture?
2. **Dependency Review** — No circular or missing dependencies?
3. **Interface Review** — Interfaces match existing contracts?
4. **Engineering Specification Review** — Concrete enough to implement?
5. **Implementation Readiness Review** — All prerequisites met?
6. **Freeze Review** — Ready to freeze? Version assigned?

**Optimize for intelligence depth, not document quantity.**
